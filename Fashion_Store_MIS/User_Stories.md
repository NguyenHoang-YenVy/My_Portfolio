# User Stories

## Store Manager

### US01 – View Business Reports

**As a** Store Manager, **I want to** view sales, inventory, promotion, and customer service reports, **so that** I can monitor store performance and make better business decisions.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- The system displays sales reports.
- The system displays inventory reports.
- The system displays promotion effectiveness.
- The system displays customer service reports.

---

### US02 – Manage Products

**As a** Store Manager, **I want to** manage product information, **so that** product data remains accurate and up to date.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- The manager can add product information.
- The manager can update product information.
- The system stores the updated product information.

---

## Sales Staff

### US03 – Create Customer Order

**As a** Sales Staff member, **I want to** create a customer order, **so that** I can record the customer's purchase accurately.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- The sales staff can select a customer.
- The sales staff can add products to the order.
- The system calculates the order total.
- The system saves the order information.

---

## Cashier

### US04 – Process Payment

**As a** Cashier, **I want to** process customer payments using available payment methods, **so that** the customer's order can be completed.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- The cashier can select a payment method.
- The system records the payment information.
- The system confirms the payment status.

---

### US05 – Generate Sales Invoice

**As a** Cashier, **I want to** generate a sales invoice after successful payment, **so that** the customer receives a record of the transaction.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- An invoice can only be generated after order confirmation.
- Payment must be completed where applicable.
- The invoice contains order and payment information.

---

## Storekeeper

### US06 – Control Inventory

**As a** Storekeeper, **I want to** monitor and control inventory activities, **so that** stock information remains accurate.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- The storekeeper can view inventory information.
- The storekeeper can review stock receipts and issues.
- The storekeeper can approve inventory transactions.

---

## Warehouse Staff

### US07 – Receive Goods

**As a** Warehouse Staff member, **I want to** record received goods, **so that** inventory quantities can be updated accurately.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- The warehouse staff can record received products.
- The actual quantity is recorded.
- Inventory quantity is updated after confirmation.

---

### US08 – Issue Goods

**As a** Warehouse Staff member, **I want to** record issued goods, **so that** stock quantities remain up to date.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- The warehouse staff can select products to issue.
- The issued quantity is recorded.
- Inventory quantity is updated after confirmation.

---

## Delivery Staff

### US09 – Manage Delivery

**As a** Delivery Staff member, **I want to** create and update delivery orders, **so that** I can monitor the delivery process.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- The delivery staff can create a delivery order.
- The delivery status can be updated.
- The system records the delivery status.

---

## Customer Service Staff

### US10 – Handle Customer Complaints

**As a** Customer Service Staff member, **I want to** record and handle customer complaints, **so that** customer issues can be followed up and resolved.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- The staff can record complaint information.
- The staff can update complaint status.
- The system stores complaint history.
