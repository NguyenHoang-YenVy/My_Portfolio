# Fashion Store Management Information System

## Project Overview

This academic group project focuses on analyzing and designing a Management Information System for **Co MayCa**, a fashion retail store. The system aims to streamline business operations across multiple departments, including Sales, Inventory, Delivery, Customer Service and Store Management.

The project covers business requirement analysis, system analysis, database design, and user interface prototyping to support efficient retail operations.

---

## Business Problem
The fashion retail store relied on manual and disconnected processes to manage sales, inventory, customer information, deliveries and promotional activities. This resulted in data inconsistencies, inefficient coordination between departments and limited visibility into business operations, making it difficult to support timely and informed decision-making.

---

## Project Objectives
- Analyze business processes and stakeholder requirements.
- Design a centralized management information system.
- Model business processes and data flows.
- Design a relational database with integrity constraints.
- Design user interface prototypes for key business functions using Figma.
